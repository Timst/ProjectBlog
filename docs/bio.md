I've also worked at smaller companies, most recently at TerraClear, a local AgTech startup.

Personally, I'm just a guy, you know? I live with my two cats and I grow sunflowers. I have intricate spreadsheets for everything and love spending time on The Computer. I recently bought a bunch of chemistry equipment and made my own cola? I cofounded a Burning Man camp where we serve 800 crêpes in a week, based on a dry batter recipe that I painstakingly developed.

I think there's real joy to be had in *creating stuff*. It's something that everybody can do, often with a far smaller time and money investment than they might think. I find it very empowering. You think about it, you look stuff up, you experiment, and suddenly there it is, in the real world, the *thing you made*. Personally I think that's very cool.
